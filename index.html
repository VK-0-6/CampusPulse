Advanced Analytics

More Than Feedback Collection

Traditional surveys dump raw data into static spreadsheets. CampusPulse applies AI semantic understanding and deterministic mathematics across two distinct operational pipelines.

Academic Stream

Faculty Feedback Intelligence

Transforms subjective course evaluations into constructive pedagogical improvement commitments.

Nuanced Sentiment Analysis

Classifies student submissions into positive, neutral, and constructively critical signals with confidence scores.

Automated Classification

Categorizes responses under teaching clarity, pacing, coursework difficulty, and lab resources for focused analysis.

Severity Assessment

Differentiates between minor subjective preferences and critical learning roadblocks requiring pedagogical intervention.

Targeted Improvement Actions

Enables instructors to log concrete action commitments tied directly to identified student concerns.

Follow-Up Impact Comparison

Measures sentiment shifts and resolution efficacy across successive feedback cycles to verify progress.

Operational Stream

Campus Issue Intelligence

Eliminates redundant ticket backlogs through automated clustering, recurrence tracking, and proactive early warnings.

Similarity & Relationship Detection

Matches duplicate and related issues across different student reports using vector text embeddings.

Automated Issue Grouping

Aggregates duplicate reports into unified parent issue groups with AI-generated titles and operational summaries.

Temporal Recurrence Detection

Database algorithms scan historical time windows to flag chronic issues that recur across weeks or semesters.

Deterministic Priority Scoring

Weights severity, report volume, and temporal spikes into an objective 0–100 operational priority score.

Early Warning Escalation

Triggers proactive warnings for department heads before localized equipment failures become systemic disruptions.

Deterministic Integrity: CampusPulse uses Google Gemini AI for qualitative interpretation, sentiment understanding, and textual grouping. All analytical thresholds, recurrence intervals, priority scores, and security policies are computed deterministically via PostgreSQL database logic.
